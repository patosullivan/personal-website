Patrick O’Sullivan

I build software that lets people own and run their own digital lives.

I’m a software engineer at Tlon, where I’ve worked since 2022. I was a founding frontend engineer on Tlon Messenger, building the original web app alongside a colleague and later bringing it to iOS and Android.

More recently, I’ve been working on making personal AI agents part of Tlon Messenger. Every user gets an agent, and multiplayer use is built in: people and their agents can share conversations and work together. The software is open source, and you can run it yourself. I’m interested in what becomes possible when an agent belongs to you and can participate in your life with other people.

Before Tlon, I worked at Headway on web and mobile applications for Breakthrough Fuel, Spotify, Thrivent, Ditto, and Ritual. Those projects ranged from logistics and financial-planning software to a relationship app and tools for in-store employees. I worked on larger teams, built applications as a sole developer, and served as a technical lead.
